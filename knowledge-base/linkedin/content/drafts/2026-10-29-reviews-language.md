78 Google reviews. 4.9 stars. Leader in our area.

And almost no Polish customers.

We run an EV service in Chorzów, Poland. When I read every review, I found the problem:

58 reviews had text.
→ 41 in Russian or Ukrainian
→ 16 in English and other languages
→ 1 in Polish

Two things happen when your reviews are in the "wrong" language:

1. Google reads review language as a relevance signal. Our profile looked irrelevant to Polish searches.
2. A Polish customer opens the profile, sees a wall of Cyrillic and thinks: "this place isn't for me."

Great service. Wrong signal.

What we're changing:
→ Ask every Polish customer for a review — in Polish, right after the car is picked up
→ Send a short pre-written prompt they can edit, not a blank field
→ Reply to reviews in the language of the market we want

Check yours today: open your Google Business profile and count reviews by language. Does it match the customers you want — or the ones you already have?

## Слайды

1. **Cover:** Audit your Meta ads in 60 minutes. 10 checks.
2. **Campaign goal.** Want leads? Use the Leads objective. "Traffic" and "Sales" buy clicks, not contacts.
3. **Placements.** Exclude Audience Network, in-stream and rewarded video. Ours: $368 per lead.
4. **Platform split.** Compare Instagram vs Facebook cost per result. Shift budget to the winner.
5. **Age & gender.** Breakdown → Age, Gender. Cut the most expensive group first.
6. **Creative fatigue.** Frequency above 3 and rising CPL = time to refresh.
7. **Format.** Run image AND video. Rotate. Don't let one creative carry the account.
8. **Numbers you report.** Billing total ≠ delivery total. Pick one and stick to it.
9. **Lead ≠ customer.** Connect leads to sales in a CRM, or your CPL lies to you.

I pulled 3 years of our Meta ads into one database.

659 ads. 12,142 leads. 27.5M impressions.

Here are 5 things I wish someone had told me in year one:

1. Instant Forms beat your website.
Campaigns sending people to the site barely produced leads. ~$3,900 went to "Sales" and "Traffic" goals that brought almost nothing. Lead form campaigns: $1–4 per lead.

2. Turn off Audience Network. Manually.
$1,103 spent → 3 leads. That's $368 per lead. It's switched on by default.

3. Instagram was cheaper than Facebook.
$3.67 vs $4.13 per lead. Small gap, but on a big budget it's real money.

4. Your core audience is narrower than you think.
Men 25–54 brought most results. 65+ was the most expensive group by far.

5. Cost per lead goes up every year if you don't refresh creatives.
Ours went from ~$2.5 to $5–6. Same product, same market. Ad fatigue is real.

How to check your own account in 10 minutes:
Ads Manager → Breakdown → By Delivery → Placement. Sort by cost per result. Anything 5× above average — exclude it.
